# Project Brief: Centralized Bus Terminal Site Selection in Abeokuta
## 1. Project Title
Centralized Bus Terminal Site Selection in Abeokuta, Ogun State, Nigeria Using GIS-Based Spatial Analysis
## 2. Background
Abeokuta is an important urban centre in Ogun State with increasing population, mobility demand, commercial activities, and movement of passengers and vehicles.
The distribution of bus stops and informal passenger loading points can contribute to traffic congestion, inefficient passenger movement, inadequate service coverage, and conflicts with other urban land uses.
A centralized bus terminal could improve public transport organization and accessibility if it is located in an appropriate area.
This project therefore applies Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and spatial analysis techniques to identify and evaluate potential locations for a centralized bus terminal in Abeokuta.
## 3. Problem Statement
The absence of a systematically evaluated location for a centralized bus terminal may contribute to inefficient public transport operations, increased travel distances, congestion, and inadequate accessibility for passengers.
There is therefore a need to identify locations that provide good accessibility and potential passenger demand while avoiding environmentally sensitive and unsuitable areas.

## 4. Main Research Question

Where should a centralized bus terminal be located in Abeokuta to maximize accessibility, passenger demand, and service coverage while minimizing congestion, travel distance, and environmental risk?

## 5. Project Aim

The aim of this project is to identify and rank suitable locations for a centralized bus terminal in Abeokuta using GIS-based spatial analysis and Multi-Criteria Evaluation (MCE).

## 6. Objectives

The project will:

1. Map the spatial distribution of existing bus stops and relevant transport facilities.
2. Assess accessibility using the existing road network.
3. Examine population distribution as an indicator of potential passenger demand.
4. Identify major activity and traffic-generating centres.
5. Evaluate land-use suitability for terminal development.
6. Identify environmental constraints and exclusion areas.
7. Integrate the selected criteria using Multi-Criteria Evaluation.
8. Identify and rank candidate locations for a centralized bus terminal.
9. Assess the potential service coverage of the selected candidate sites.

## 7. Study Area

The study focuses on Abeokuta, Ogun State, Nigeria.

The analysis will consider the urban area and its surrounding zones that are relevant to passenger movement, road accessibility, and potential bus-terminal development.

## 9. Methodology

The project will involve the following major stages:

### Stage 1: Data Collection

Relevant spatial datasets will be collected from field observations, OpenStreetMap, GRID3, SRTM, Sentinel-2, and other appropriate sources.

### Stage 2: Data Preparation

The datasets will be cleaned, projected into a common coordinate reference system, clipped to the study area, and prepared for spatial analysis.

### Stage 3: Spatial Analysis

Individual criteria will be analysed to determine their influence on potential bus-terminal locations.

The analysis will include:

- Road accessibility
- Distance from existing bus stops
- Population distribution
- Proximity to major activity centres
- Land-use suitability
- Environmental constraints
- Service-area analysis

### Stage 4: Multi-Criteria Evaluation

The selected criteria will be standardized and combined using a weighted Multi-Criteria Evaluation (MCE) approach.

Areas that satisfy the required suitability conditions will receive higher suitability scores, while constrained areas will receive lower scores or be excluded.

### Stage 5: Candidate-Site Identification

High-suitability areas will be examined to identify practical candidate locations.

Candidate sites will be evaluated based on accessibility, potential passenger demand, service coverage, land-use compatibility, and environmental considerations.

## 13. Expected Impact

The project is intended to provide a spatial decision-support framework that can assist transport planners and relevant authorities in evaluating potential locations for a centralized bus terminal in Abeokuta.

The analysis can also provide a reproducible GIS workflow for evaluating transport infrastructure location using accessibility, demand, land-use, and environmental criteria.
